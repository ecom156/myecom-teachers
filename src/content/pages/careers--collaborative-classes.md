**“ECOM Cultures” Program is helping Japanese schools to find online cultural exchange partner schools.**

We are looking for schools outside of Japan that are interested in collaboration with Japanese schools through online classes.

If you are a school teacher or know someone who is,  
please consider introducing your class to this program.

The students of both sides prepare presentations and share them in an online class.

Both sides can experience the excitement of intercultural learning and inspire each other by meeting peers from different cultures!

**Details:**

-   **Compensation for teachers:** **$50 USD** per class (45 minutes).

Typically, multiple classes are conducted, so for example, conducting 3 classes would result in $150 USD compensation.　  
Payment can be made via PayPal or bank transfer (Wise).

-   **Online tools**: Teams or Google Meet  
    

-   **Eligibility:**  
    School teachers for grades K5-12 (upper elementary to high school).  
    We are seeking schools that can conduct classes between 8:30 AM and 4:30 PM Japan Standard Time.  
    

-   **Class Content:**  
    Group presentations in English about each other’s countries, schools, and local areas.  
    (Schools studying Japanese can present in Japanese if preferred.)  
    

-   **How to Apply:**  
    Registration is through a form. After a simple initial application,  
    we will contact you to arrange an online interview.  
    If the schedule doesn’t work or you become too busy, you can decline at that time.

We encourage all school teachers who are interested to apply.

Please do not hesitate to contact us for further details.

-   **Current High-Demand Regions:**  
    • New Zealand  
    • Australia  
    • Guam Schools

Of course other countries are also welcome to apply.

We look forward to your application!
